# Arena Tweaks

A set of (user)scripts that enhance Arena.AI's user interface and user experience in various ways.

## Arena Sidebar Tweaks

**Script:** `arena-sidebar-tweaks.user.js`

The most prevalent features added by this script are:

1. Grouping based on custom folders or collections
2. Various other automatically sorted options 
   - By arena type/modality (mostly broken for now)
   - By Last activity (analogous to the stock sidebar)
   - By calendar month
   - And optionally: Selecting the "native" option to restore the original sidebar listing, unmodified.
3. The title of the conversation can now be expanded in legibility upon hovering over the list entry. Why isn't this a thing in the official interface??
4. A variety of ways to manage or organise your custom folders
5. The option of defining auto-matching collections based on patterns or regular expressions.
6. Pineapple fruit.
7. Introduces support for collapsible categories ("menu-groups"), which was not present in the official interface.
8. Near future: Easier exporting of conversations by permitting users to click an export button in the three dots menu
   - Ah, yeah. About that. I forgot to add the three dots menu so (for now) it's kinda difficult to archive your sessions.
